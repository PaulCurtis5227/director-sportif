Slots
Look
One slot per meter, word or basic, in this order (left to right, then down, with more than one column). "word": the meter's word under the number. Yellow / red: the number turns that color at those values; empty = the meter's own colors. The Grid only shows what the Director Sportif window publishes: keep that window open (bike, CdA and calls are set there).
Speeds and distances follow Sauce's metric / imperial setting.
