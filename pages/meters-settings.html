How each call reaches you: a chime (each type has its own), your computer's voice, or both; none = only the windows (the Director Sportif window, the Grid's Director tile). One type sounds at most every 30 s.
The course facts of a race (scored sprints and KOMs, arches and what they give). The one for the route you're racing is used by itself; here you set your plan for each.
Recordings go to Downloads as ds-capture-….ndjson.gz. For other riders only where they were and the draft they got, under a random id that's new for every recording (no names, power, heart rate or body data); for you, everything. They're stored every 30 s while recording, so a crash or a closed window loses at most 30 s: the next time this window opens, it saves them.
If you record or stream your rides with OBS: the Director Sportif writes when your recording and your stream started into its recording, so a report can link each moment to your video (and, later, to everyone's videos that had you in view). In OBS: Tools ▸ WebSocket Server Settings ▸ enable it. The password stays in this window's settings on this computer.
Rehearse: with no race (not in an event, OBS not recording), the Director Sportif window shows the title card, starts OBS, records 90 s and stops OBS, so you can check it all in your video. The title uses the card picked on the Race card tab. Keep the Director Sportif window open.
This window: the call now and a short status. The meters, their layout and colors are set on the Grid (its own gear).
